Your video. Your headline. Your team.

For video: click Add video, position your text and press Export MP4. New widescreen videos automatically use Blur background / full video: the full picture stays sharp over a moving, blurred copy. Adjust blur strength, background darkness and video vertical position in the Video source panel. The blur is included in the exported MP4, not just the preview. Use Fill to crop to 9:16, or Fit to show the full picture with black bars. The default output is 1080 x 1920. Original file audio is included unless you turn it off. Preview mute never changes export audio.

Exports run in real time using native browser recording. Keep this tab visible and your device awake. Long clips, high-resolution sources and 60 fps can strain slower devices; use 720p / 30 fps as needed. Start and end points are approximate, not frame-accurate. The export panel shows your browser's MP4/audio support. AAC is preferred; a clearly labeled Opus fallback is available when necessary. No WebM is renamed as MP4.

Branding & sponsor: your supplied NBC Sports Philadelphia logo is built in. Switch it on or off in the Branding & sponsor panel. Add a separate sponsor PNG, JPG or WebP; it is centered directly beneath the NBC logo. Enable White background behind sponsor to add a white card, then adjust its padding and corner roundness. This does not remove an existing background from a logo file. Use a transparent PNG for the cleanest edges. Drag either logo to move the stack; use the corner handle to scale both, or use separate width sliders. Logo proportions are preserved. Branding stays on for the entire clip unless switched off. Both MP4 and PNG exports contain visible logos.

Text timing: select a layer and uncheck Show for the entire clip. Times use the original video's clock. PNG exports ignore timing. Video files are not embedded in saved designs; reopen the same file after loading a design to restore its trim.

  1. Choose Sixers, Flyers, Eagles, or Phillies. The selected layer takes that team's palette.
  2. Type your headline. Use Enter for line breaks, *asterisks* to highlight words, and choose a banner style. Fonts use the typefaces available on your device.
  3. Drag your banner in the preview. Resize with the corner handle or text-size slider. Add more text layers for separate callouts.
  4. Keep Transparent / overlay only selected and export a full-size 1080 × 1920 PNG. Checkerboards, guides, and selection handles never appear in the file.

Add a reference photo to line things up. It is only a preview unless you select Include reference photo. Local autosave stores the text, logos and settings; Save design also keeps your logos and reference image in an editable JSON file.
